About Wayfarer

We built the guide we wished existed before our own first long trip.

Our story

Wayfarer started in 2021 as a shared notes document between three friends who kept sending each other the same question: "where should we go next?" What began as a messy list of beaches, temples, and countries slowly turned into this site — a place to search by feeling, not just by destination name.

What we believe

Good travel recommendations aren't rankings — they're honest descriptions of what a place actually feels like to stand in. We favour specific, sensory detail over superlatives, and we'd rather list three places well than thirty places badly.

How we choose what to list

Firsthand

Every recommendation is written by someone who has actually been there, not summarised from a listicle.

Specific

We describe what a place is actually like — the light, the crowds, the best time to arrive — not just that it's "beautiful."

Unranked

We don't do "top 10" lists. A quiet temple and a famous one can both be worth the trip, for different reasons.
